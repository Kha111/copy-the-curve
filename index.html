Copy the curve

Your job: build an expression that copies a mystery curve as closely as you can, near x = 0.

You'll do it in three stages. Each stage zooms further out.

Team rules

  • One phone per team.
  • Talk it through and agree before you tap.
  • The shaded area is the error, the gap between the curve and your expression. Make it as small as you can.

Last step

Look back at what you built

    What pattern do you spot?

    Agree on an answer as a team, then tell your facilitator.

    • What's the error at x = 0 every time?
    • Where does each new term make the biggest difference?
    • Look at the signs and the numbers in each term. What comes next?
